01

Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our services, ensure platform security, and deliver a better experience for our users. The types of information we collect include:

Account Information

Information you provide when creating or managing an account, such as your name, email address, and authentication details.

Usage Information

Information about how you interact with our platform, including pages viewed, features used, device information, and technical logs.

Communication Information

Information you provide when contacting our team, requesting support, discussing partnerships, or communicating with us.

Technical Information

Certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and system logs, to help maintain platform security, performance, and reliability.

02

How We Use Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain ChainXGrowth and its features.
  • Improve our intelligence systems, platform features, and user experience.
  • Understand platform usage and identify technical issues.
  • Protect our systems, users, and information against unauthorized activity.
  • Communicate with users regarding services, updates, support, and relevant information.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
03

AI, Analytics & Automated Systems

ChainXGrowth may use automated systems, analytics, and AI technologies to process information and improve platform functionality.

Where applicable, information may be processed to generate insights, improve system performance, detect patterns, or provide relevant intelligence. We aim to use such technologies responsibly and with appropriate safeguards.

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation and improvement of our services without an appropriate legal basis or permission where required.
